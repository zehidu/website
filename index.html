The 90-day experiment

Instrument first. Publish second. Learn continuously.

Ranking is an outcome of usefulness, clarity, crawlability, and time—not a switch hidden in a dashboard.

Day 0

Recover control

Map the registrar, DNS, host, source repo, search ownership, public files, and deployment path.

Days 1–14

Make it measurable

Connect analytics, publish the sitemap, remove the fake dashboard, and establish truthful baselines.

Days 15–45

Build search surface

Publish tightly related guides, answer long-tail questions, and strengthen internal links between useful pages.

Days 46–90

Follow the evidence

Use queries, impressions, engagement, and real-user performance to improve pages that show early demand.

What counts as proof

No vanity traffic. No mystery rankings. No fictional customers.

Leading indicators

Indexed pages, non-branded impressions, useful queries, crawl health, click-through rate, and engaged visits show whether the site is earning discoverability.

Outcome indicators

Qualified actions, returning visitors, helpful page paths, links earned, and durable organic clicks show whether discoverability is becoming value.
