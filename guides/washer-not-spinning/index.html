Washer 5-step visual guide

Washer not spinning?

Separate settings, balance, and drainage from a real drive-system fault.

!
Stop immediately

Violent movement, hot or electrical smell, leaking near wiring, or a trapped locked door.

1. Keep guards intact

Never bypass the lid or door lock.

Disconnect power before touching a hose, leveling feet, or any area the owner manual identifies as user-accessible.

2. Test the easy causes first

  1. Read cycle and spin speed. Delicate, hand-wash, low-spin, or no-spin settings leave more water in fabrics.
  2. Redistribute the load. A bulky item, overload, or bunched bedding can trigger imbalance protection.
  3. Look for standing water. Many machines will not fully spin until they drain. Use the drain path first.
  4. Confirm the machine is level. Follow the installation guide; rocking feet encourage repeated imbalance.
  5. Record one controlled test. Save the cycle, code, lock behavior, basket movement, and sound for service.

3. Read the boundary

Setting changed itOperating choice
Rebalancing fixed itLoad issue
Still no spinDiagnosis needed

A persistent no-spin can involve the lock, drive components, motor, speed sensing, wiring, or controls. Only model-specific diagnosis can separate them.
