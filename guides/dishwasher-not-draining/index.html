Dishwasher Symptom guide

Dishwasher not draining?

Standing water often begins with a blocked filter or drain path, but glass and sharp debris make even a simple inspection worth approaching carefully.

Stop and get help if

Water is near electrical wiring, a leak continues with the supply closed, the breaker trips, or access requires live testing or removal of a fixed panel.

Cut power before reaching inside

The control button is not a power disconnect.

Switch off the circuit breaker or unplug the unit, close its water supply, wear cut-resistant gloves, and consult the exact model manual before removing a filter or pump cover.

Move through the drain path

  1. Confirm the cycle completed. Cancel or reset only as the manual directs, then listen during the drain portion and record any code.
  2. Remove standing water safely. Use a cup, sponge, and shallow container. Do not reach blindly; broken glass can hide below cloudy water.
  3. Clean the documented filter. Follow the owner manual for removal and reseating. An incorrectly seated filter can create another performance problem.
  4. Inspect visible hose routing. Look under the sink for a kink, crushing, a blocked air gap, or a connection affected by recent disposal work. Do not detach plumbing unless you are prepared to contain wastewater and restore the connection correctly.
  5. Stop at the pump boundary. Only inspect a pump cover if the model manual explicitly identifies it as an owner procedure. Persistent humming, grinding, silence during drain, or recurring water warrants service.

What the check tells you

Debris in a filter or visible air gap points toward maintenance. A recently replaced disposal can change the sink connection. A clear path with continued standing water can involve the pump, wiring, controls, or internal hose—none can be confirmed from the symptom alone.

Price the diagnosis, not a guessed part

Ask for a complete estimate and repair warranty. If repair is substantial, compare it with the dishwasher’s age and a comparable installed replacement price in the decision calculator.
