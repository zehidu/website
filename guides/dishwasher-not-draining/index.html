1. Cut real power

The control button is not a disconnect.

Switch off the breaker or unplug, close the water supply, wear cut-resistant gloves, and use the exact model manual.

2. Move through the drain path

  1. Confirm the cycle completed. Cancel or reset only as the manual directs; record any code.
  2. Remove standing water safely. Use a cup, sponge, and shallow container. Never reach blindly through cloudy water.
  3. Clean and reseat the documented filter. An incorrectly seated filter can create another problem.
  4. Inspect visible hose routing. Look for kinks, crushing, a blocked air gap, or a disposal connection changed by recent work.
  5. Stop at the pump boundary. Persistent humming, grinding, silence during drain, or recurring water warrants service.

A clear visible path with standing water can involve the pump, wiring, controls, or an internal hose. The symptom alone cannot identify which.
