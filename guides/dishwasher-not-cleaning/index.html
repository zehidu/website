Dishwasher Symptom guide

Dishwasher not cleaning well?

Poor cleaning can come from blocked water paths, loading, maintenance, detergent, or cycle choice. Test those variables before assuming the machine has reached the end of its life.

Do not mix chemicals

Use only products approved by the manufacturer. Never combine dishwasher cleaners, bleach, ammonia, vinegar, or other chemicals.

Read the pattern on the dishes

Before rerunning the load, note whether residue appears on one rack or everywhere, whether the detergent dissolved, and whether spray arms can rotate. A localized pattern can reveal a blocked arm or poor loading; a whole-load problem points toward water, detergent, filter, or cycle conditions.

Change one variable at a time

  1. Reload for spray access. Keep tall items from blocking spray-arm rotation and avoid nesting utensils or overlapping bowls.
  2. Clean and correctly reseat the filter. Follow the model manual. A dirty or incorrectly installed filter can allow food to recirculate or reduce performance.
  3. Inspect spray-arm holes. With power disconnected, remove an arm only if the owner manual describes it. Rinse it and clear visible debris without enlarging the openings.
  4. Use automatic-dishwasher detergent correctly. Check freshness, storage, the dispenser, dose, water hardness, and cycle guidance from the manufacturer. Never use hand dish soap.
  5. Confirm hot-water readiness and cycle selection. Follow the model instructions for incoming water and heated options. A short or energy-saving cycle may not suit heavily soiled items.
  6. Run a controlled test load. Use normally soiled dishes, documented loading, a clean filter, and one recommended detergent dose. If the result remains poor, record which rack and items were affected.

When maintenance is not enough

A spray arm that receives no water, detergent that never releases, persistently cool operation, unusual noise, codes, or poor results after a controlled test can involve circulation, fill, heating, sensing, or control systems. Those need model-specific diagnosis.

Do not replace it for a loading problem

First establish that maintenance and use conditions are controlled. If a technician identifies a component failure, compare the complete estimate and warranty with an installed replacement using the repair-or-replace calculator.
