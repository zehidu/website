Plain language Last updated August 26, 2026

Measure the product, not the person.

The Renew Up needs reliable evidence to improve. That does not require collecting typed cost values, model serial numbers, names, or a hidden cross-site identity.

Current measurement status

No third-party analytics service is connected. Anonymous QA events are stored only in this browser tab’s session storage and are not transmitted.

What happens on this site now

The site creates a random identifier for the current browser tab and records allowed product events—such as a page view, calculator completion, or symptom search—inside session storage. Closing the tab normally ends that session. The event log is for quality testing and is not sent to The Renew Up, Google, Cloudflare, Microsoft, or another provider.

Calculator age and cost inputs remain in page memory while the page is open. They are not written to the event log or sent anywhere.

The planned production measurement stack

  • Google Search Console for search queries, impressions, clicks, position, crawl, and indexing.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics for lightweight aggregate traffic and performance.
  • Google Analytics 4 for consent-aware product events and acquisition paths.
  • BigQuery for durable event history, content versions, and experiment analysis.
  • Sampled session replay only after consent, with all form inputs masked and sensitive pages excluded.

The production notice and consent controls will be updated before any of those tools begin collecting data.

Meaningful events we plan to keep

Examples include landing page, anonymous visit session, acquisition channel, selected appliance category, selected symptom, calculator start and completion, broad result ranges, result type, content version, internal navigation, recall-check click, and page-performance measurements.

Broad ranges, not raw values

A calculator event can say “repair estimate was 26–40% of replacement” without storing either dollar amount.

Information we will not put into analytics

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or street addresses
  • Appliance serial numbers or text typed into forms
  • Exact repair estimates, replacement prices, or appliance ages
  • Passwords, payment details, or account credentials
  • Raw IP addresses in our own data warehouse
  • Cross-site advertising profiles created by The Renew Up

Retention and deletion

Before production measurement is enabled, this section will state concrete retention periods for raw events, aggregate reports, and any optional outcome submissions. Optional repair-outcome reports will be stored separately from analytics and will require explicit consent.

Contact boundary

The site does not currently accept personal submissions. A dedicated privacy-contact address will be added before an outcome form, account system, or analytics provider collects personal data; no placeholder address has been invented.
