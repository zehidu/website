What happens now

Cloudflare Web Analytics measures aggregate visits, page views, referrers, browser categories, countries, and real-user performance. Cloudflare says the service does not use visitors’ personal data or track individuals across its customers’ sites. Read Cloudflare’s documentation.

A separate allowlisted QA log stays in this tab’s session storage. It uses a random tab identifier, keeps at most 100 events, and is not transmitted. Closing the tab normally ends it. Exact calculator answers remain only in page memory.

Useful evidence, in broad ranges

Examples: landing page, acquisition channel, appliance category, calculator start and completion, result type, broad quote and life-used ranges, content version, internal navigation, recall click, and page speed.

Broad ranges, not raw values

A calculator event can say “repair estimate was 26–40% of replacement” without storing either dollar amount.

Never analytics fields

IdentityNames or contact detailsNo email, phone, address, or serial number.
MoneyExact costs or ageNo quote, replacement price, warranty, or exact age.
SensitiveCredentials or profilesNo passwords, payment data, or Renew Up cross-site profile.

Retention boundary

Cloudflare controls retention of its aggregate reports. The local QA log normally ends when this tab closes. The Renew Up has no analytics warehouse and accepts no outcome submissions today.

Before anything expands

This notice, consent controls, contact route, and retention periods will be updated before durable product analytics, replay, an outcome form, or an account system begins collecting data.
