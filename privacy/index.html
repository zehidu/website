Plain language Last updated August 26, 2026

Measure the product, not the person.

The Renew Up needs reliable evidence to improve. That does not require collecting typed cost values, model serial numbers, names, or a hidden cross-site identity.

Current measurement status

Cloudflare Web Analytics is active for cookie-free aggregate traffic and real-user performance measurement. Anonymous product QA events stay only in this browser tab’s session storage and are not transmitted.

What happens on this site now

A lightweight Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon measures aggregate visits, page views, referrers, browser categories, countries, and real-user performance. Cloudflare states that this service does not collect or use visitors’ personal data and does not track individual users across its customers’ sites. It does not use our calculator values, typed symptom searches, serial numbers, names, or the product QA event log. Read Cloudflare’s data-collection documentation.

Separately, the site creates a random identifier for the current browser tab and records allowlisted product QA events—such as a calculator completion or symptom search—inside session storage. Closing the tab normally ends that session. This QA log is not transmitted to The Renew Up, Google, Cloudflare, Microsoft, or another provider.

Calculator age and cost inputs remain in page memory while the page is open. They are not written to the event log or sent anywhere.

Current and possible future measurement

  • Google Search Console is active for search queries, impressions, clicks, position, crawl, and indexing.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics is active for lightweight aggregate traffic and performance.
  • Google Analytics 4 is not connected; it may later be used for consent-aware product events and acquisition paths.
  • BigQuery is not connected; it may later hold durable event history, content versions, and experiment analysis.
  • Session replay is not connected and would be considered only after consent, with all form inputs masked and sensitive pages excluded.

This notice and the site’s consent controls will be updated before any product-event analytics, data warehouse, session replay, outcome form, or account system begins collecting data.

Meaningful events we plan to keep

Examples include landing page, anonymous visit session, acquisition channel, selected appliance category, selected symptom, calculator start and completion, broad result ranges, result type, content version, internal navigation, recall-check click, and page-performance measurements.

Broad ranges, not raw values

A calculator event can say “repair estimate was 26–40% of replacement” without storing either dollar amount.

Information we will not put into analytics

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or street addresses
  • Appliance serial numbers or text typed into forms
  • Exact repair estimates, replacement prices, or appliance ages
  • Passwords, payment details, or account credentials
  • Raw IP addresses in our own data warehouse
  • Cross-site advertising profiles created by The Renew Up

Retention and deletion

Cloudflare controls retention of the aggregate Web Analytics reports in its service. The local product QA log keeps at most 100 allowlisted events in session storage and normally ends when the browser tab closes. The Renew Up does not currently operate its own analytics warehouse or accept outcome submissions. Concrete retention periods will be published before either feature is enabled.

Contact boundary

The site does not currently accept personal submissions. A dedicated privacy-contact address will be added before an outcome form, account system, or analytics provider collects personal data; no placeholder address has been invented.
