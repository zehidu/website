Plain-language notice

Privacy should be as measurable as growth.

This page states the current production status, the planned measurement layer, and the boundaries the experiment will keep.

Last updated August 26, 2026Analytics status: not yet connected

Current status

At the August 26, 2026 baseline, The Renew Up had no active analytics beacon in its public source. The rebuilt local version also does not activate an analytics service until the correct owner account is confirmed and the production deployment is ready.

The website does not currently offer an account, checkout, newsletter form, consultation form, or comment box. The old form that stored entries only in a visitor's own browser has been removed.

Planned website measurement

The planned first measurement layer is Cloudflare Web Analytics. Cloudflare describes it as privacy-first and states that it does not collect or use visitors' personal data. Once enabled, this notice will be updated to state the activation date and the deployed source will contain the site-specific beacon.

The purpose is to understand aggregate page views, referrers, general visitor patterns, and real-user performance—not to identify an individual.

Google Search Console

Search Console may report how pages appear in Google Search, including aggregate impressions, clicks, queries, countries, devices, and index coverage. Google omits or anonymizes some query data to protect privacy. Search Console does not turn this site into a user-account service.

Server and delivery logs

Infrastructure providers such as Netlify, Cloudflare, and the domain registrar may process request, security, and operational data under their own terms and privacy notices. That processing is separate from a custom visitor database controlled by The Renew Up.

External links

This site links to GitHub, Google, Cloudflare, Netlify, and other primary documentation. Following an external link sends your browser to that provider, where its privacy terms apply.

No sale of personal data

The Renew Up does not sell personal data. The current site does not request personal data through a form.

Changes to this notice

Measurement changes will be dated here and in the public source history. If the site later introduces a form, account, or paid service, this notice must be expanded before that feature goes live.

Measurement status: pending account connection. No unverified analytics numbers will be presented as live visitor data.
